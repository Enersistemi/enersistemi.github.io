Tesla Powerwall in Puglia

Tesla certified installers. Our first Powerwall dates back to 2016 — today we install them across the provinces of Brindisi, Bari, Taranto and Lecce.

The Tesla Powerwall is the battery our customers ask for most, and for good reason: 13.5 kWh of capacity, backup that keeps the house running during a power cut, and an app that shows production, consumption and reserve in real time.

We knew it before it became famous: our first Powerwall — a 6.4 kWh first generation unit — was installed in Ostuni in 2016, among the first in Puglia. Since 2017 we have been Tesla certified installers, with the official Powerwall training and direct access to Tesla's technical channels for commissioning, warranty and support.

Since then we have installed dozens across Puglia: on new systems, as retrofits on existing ones, in cascades of up to 4 units on masserie and villas, and since 2025 the new Powerwall 3 with its built-in inverter as well.

Why Powerwall

What makes it different

13.5 kWh per unit

Each Powerwall stores 13.5 kWh: the energy produced during the day is used in the evening and at night, taking self-consumption beyond 80%.

Blackout backup

With the Gateway, if the grid fails the house switches to the battery automatically, without interruption. For many of our customers in the countryside this is reason number one.

Expandable in cascade

Need more reserve? Powerwalls stack: we have installations with 2, 3 and up to 4 units (54 kWh) on masserie and villas.

Powerwall 3 with built-in inverter

Since 2025 we install the Powerwall 3: it includes the solar inverter, so on a new system no separate inverter is needed.

Retrofit on existing systems

A Powerwall can be added to a solar system already in service, even one we did not install ourselves: we do it constantly across Puglia.

Frequently asked questions

Tesla Powerwall, the answers

How much does an installed Tesla Powerwall cost?

As a guide, between €9,000 and €11,500 including VAT, turnkey, depending on the model (Powerwall 2 or 3), the Gateway and the work required. The survey is free: we give you the exact price before you decide, and battery storage qualifies for the same Italian tax deduction as solar.

Can I add a Powerwall to my existing solar system?

Yes: the Powerwall 2 connects on the AC side to any existing system, even one we did not install, with an inverter of any brand (Fronius, SolarEdge, Huawei...). It is one of the jobs we do most often.

Does the Powerwall power the house during a blackout?

Yes. With the Backup Gateway, when the grid goes down the house switches to the battery automatically in a fraction of a second, and the solar system keeps recharging it. That is the main difference compared with many competing batteries.

How long have you been installing Tesla Powerwalls?

Our first Powerwall — a 6.4 kWh first generation unit — was installed in Ostuni in 2016, among the first in Puglia. We have been Tesla certified installers since 2017 and today we install Powerwall 2 and Powerwall 3 across the provinces of Brindisi, Bari, Taranto and Lecce.

How many Powerwalls does my house need?

For a typical home one is enough (13.5 kWh). For villas, masserie or high consumption several units are installed in cascade: on our sites we have gone up to four Powerwalls, for 54 kWh of storage.

Powerwall 2 or Powerwall 3?

It depends on the starting point: on an existing solar system the Powerwall 2 is usually the better choice (it connects on the AC side without touching anything); on a new system the Powerwall 3 includes the inverter and simplifies everything.
