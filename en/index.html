Frequently asked questions

The answers you are looking for

How much does a solar system for a house cost?

It depends on your consumption and your roof: as a guide, a turnkey 3–6 kWp domestic system starts at around €6,000, battery excluded. The survey and the quote are free and without obligation: we give you the exact cost and the estimated saving before you decide.

What tax relief and incentives can I claim?

Domestic solar falls under the Italian home-renovation tax deduction (in 2026: 50% on your main residence, recovered over 10 years) and benefits from reduced VAT at 10%. The rules change from year to year: we handle all the paperwork so you get the maximum.

How long does it take from quote to a working system?

The installation itself takes 1–3 days. Including the grid connection paperwork and activation, the system is usually up and running within 4–8 weeks of signing the quote.

Is it worth adding a battery such as the Tesla Powerwall?

If you are often out during the day, yes: with a battery you use your own energy in the evening and at night too, taking self-consumption from 30–40% to over 80%. We have been Tesla Powerwall certified installers since 2017 and we also install Huawei, SolarEdge and Fronius batteries.

Does solar work during a power cut?

The panels on their own do not: by law the system shuts down when the grid goes off. With a battery system that includes backup, such as the Tesla Powerwall, the house keeps running automatically even during an outage.

Do you handle the GSE paperwork and Italian bureaucracy?

Yes, all included: the connection application to the distributor, GSE paperwork (net metering / dedicated withdrawal), customs notifications where required, and the documents you need for the tax deduction. You sign, we do the rest.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Ostuni (BR) and work across central and southern Puglia: the provinces of Brindisi, Bari, Taranto and Lecce. Since 2008 we have completed more than 420 installations, from Ostuni and the Valle d'Itria down into Salento.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Very little: we monitor production remotely, and we recommend a periodic check with panel cleaning to keep output at its best. We also service systems that were installed by someone else.
